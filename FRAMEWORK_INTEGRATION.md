├── Imaginary Order: Self-image based on social reflections

├── Natural Style: Innate behavioral tendencies

The system analyzes your responses across all frameworks to identify:

- **Johari Patterns**: What themes appear in both self and other perspectives?
- **Relational Patterns**: How do you describe connection and relationship?
- **Authenticity Markers**: When do you feel most/least genuine?
- **Behavioral Indicators**: What DISC tendencies show up in your stories?
- **Existential Themes**: How do you create meaning and make choices?

### **STEP 2: INTEGRATION ANALYSIS**
The engine then combines these patterns to identify:

- **Primary Archetype**: Your unique combination of traits (e.g., "The Conscious Relationalist")
- **Developmental Edge**: Where you're ready to grow next
- **Integration Opportunities**: How frameworks can work together
- **Shadow Areas**: What you might be avoiding or not seeing

### **STEP 3: HOLISTIC RECOMMENDATIONS**
Finally, it generates integrated practices that work across all frameworks:

**Example Integration Practice:**
```
DAILY FRAMEWORK CHECK-IN:
Each evening, reflect on:

1. JOHARI: "What did I learn about myself through others today?"
2. BUBER: "How authentically did I show up in relationships?"
3. LACAN: "What unconscious patterns did I notice?"
4. DISC: "How did my behavioral style serve or limit me?"
5. EXISTENTIAL: "What choices reflected my authentic values?"

This creates a comprehensive growth practice that develops 
all dimensions simultaneously.
```
